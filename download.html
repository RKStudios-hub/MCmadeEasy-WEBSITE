Safety of our launcher

We are aware that the security of our users' computers and data is of utmost importance. Therefore, we can assure you that using MCmadeEasy poses no threat. Previous false alarms have largely been resolved by reaching out to antivirus companies, which have re-scanned our files and affirmed our safety. However, it's important to note that occasional false positives may still occur, as antivirus programs can sometimes misinterpret the behavior of our software. These instances are usually rectified within a week.
